# Privacy Policy for BucketAudit

**Effective Date: June 10, 2026**

Welcome to **BucketAudit**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how BucketAudit collects, uses, processes, and safeguards your information when you use our personal finance management application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal and Financial Data (Manual Entry)
BucketAudit is designed to help you monitor spending, manage budgets, and maintain a digital ledger. The app collects and stores the information you manually enter, including:
* **Expense Records:** Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and recipient/merchant names.
* **Budgeting Parameters:** Daily and monthly budget limits.
* **Digital Ledger ("Give & Take"):** Names or contact profiles of individuals you lend money to or borrow money from, along with corresponding receivable and payable amounts.
---

## 2. Data Storage, Security, and Cloud Transfer

### A. On-Device Storage (Room Database)
Your privacy is built into the architecture of BucketAudit. All financial records, transaction histories, ledger details, and extracted SMS data are stored securely in a local, on-device database (Room DB).

### B. No Cloud Synchronization
**BucketAudit does not use automatic cloud synchronization.** Your financial data remains strictly on your physical device and is not transmitted to, stored on, or synced with any central server or external cloud database. 

### C. Data Control & Exporting
You maintain absolute control over your data. BucketAudit provides an optional feature allowing you to manually export your financial records (e.g., into a CSV format) to your local storage or to share it via channels you choose.

---

BucketAudit integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) to sustain operations and provide specific functionalities. These third parties may collect information according to their respective privacy policies:

### Google AdMob
We display advertisements within the app using Google AdMob. To personalize and measure ad performance, AdMob may automatically collect certain device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), device type, and basic telemetry. 

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

BucketAudit does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete it from our local structures.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected inside the application with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
